MODEL NOTES

Transparent by design.

The regional score combines temperature (25%), low humidity (30%), wind (20%), and seven-day dryness (25%). It is a starting heuristic, not a calibrated scientific fire-weather index.

The 6h and 24h cones use a generic base spread rate adjusted by wind. Terrain, fuel, moisture, suppression, and spotting are not represented.

Use Pyra to explore global patterns—not to make safety decisions.
